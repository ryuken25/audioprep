## What they make

A vocal cover singer in the Japanese "utaite" (歌ってみた) tradition, posting
short covers as video on X and TikTok, plus full covers on YouTube. Songs
seen on the timeline: syh「海へ」(from the film *Given*), mosawo "Fuyu no
Present" and "Aitai", King Gnu "Ame Sansan" (a duet with @Zeikou_Ch,
illustration by @nkcllg), Saucy Dog "Sugar". Posts mix Indonesian, English
and Japanese in the same breath ("A-A-A-ARIGATOU GOZAIMUCHHH", "Entah kenapa
upload cover di x sama tiktok pecah bgt").

Two posts matter for this product:

- "Entah kenapa upload cover di x sama tiktok pecah bgt jdnya, soon reupload"
  (why do my covers come out crushed on X and TikTok). That is the problem
  Kenshi AudioPrep exists to fix.
- "testing my converter" on a syh「海へ」short cover, posted the same day the
  app shipped. The tool is already in the workflow.

Also: "Day 1 belajar mixing, mohon doakan supaya konsisten" (day one of
learning to mix). The user is learning audio, not an engineer. UI copy can
explain, but must not talk down.
